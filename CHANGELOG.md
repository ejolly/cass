- Add quiz authoring, --config, and course-local times; split canvas CLI and client

Quiz files (TOML) round-trip through export and create --from; the client
posts settings and questions, re-saves so Canvas recounts, and rolls back a
partial quiz on failure. Concluded courses fall back to /statistics for
questions. sync handles [[canvas.quizzes]] and --create-groups.

Global --config PATH / CASS_CONFIG points at another cass.toml and resolves
credentials beside it. Naive times are read in the course time zone via
zoneinfo (pytz dropped); cass init records time_zone.

cass/cli/canvas.py and cass/apis/canvas/client.py become packages with no
behavior change; help output is identical. ([d197672](https://github.com/ejolly/cass/commit/d197672cce84517c1c2645e100e33fb09acf4f61))
